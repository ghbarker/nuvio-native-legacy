Nuvio TV/Mobile Test Logs99 — Sports league cards

Install as Update over Nuvio TV/Mobile Test. Do not uninstall or clear data.

1. Open Sports, then All. Your combined Favorites match row stays at the top. Each sport below it now has a row of league cards with logos, rather than loading every league's matches.
2. Scroll down several sports, then back up. League cards should stay in place without fetching scoreboards as you browse. Swipe a sport row sideways or use Left/Right on a remote to reach its leagues.
3. Open a league. Its matches should appear in the existing match-card layout. Tap a match, choose a confirmed channel in Sources, and verify playback starts.
4. Go Back to Sports. The same sport row, league selection, horizontal position and vertical position should be retained. Reopen the league within a minute: its recent matches should appear without another loading screen.
5. On a league's matches page, leave it open for a minute to refresh scores. Existing cards should remain visible while updating. A failed update should keep the previous cards with the existing error message.
6. In Sports → Leagues, turn one league off and return. Its card should disappear. Turn it back on to restore it. Sports on/off choices should also continue working.
7. Tap a Favorites match to open Sources. Long-press a match to open team-favorite options; releasing should not open Sources. League cards open their match list directly.
8. Optional: repeat navigation with a TV remote and in phone portrait/landscape. The left sidebar should not move the Sports content.

If anything reloads unexpectedly, save a fresh app log after reproducing it. The new [esportes] “abrir liga” line identifies recent-cache reuse or a match refresh using only the public league ID and item counts.
