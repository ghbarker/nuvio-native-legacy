Nuvio TV/Mobile Test Logs104

Install over your existing Nuvio TV/Mobile Test app. Do not uninstall it.

1. Open Sports > American football > National Football League.
2. Open the league containing a Carolina Panthers match (the Panthers vs Buccaneers card from your screenshot is suitable if still listed).
3. The Panthers logo should retain its blue and black facial details, instead of becoming a solid white silhouette.
4. Check the other team logos on the same screen. Coloured artwork should keep its colours, while genuinely single-tone dark artwork remains visible.
5. Open another sport and verify its logos and sport-specific missing-image defaults still display normally.

Validation includes a real Panthers PNG rendered by the shared SDL/GLES UI, regression checks for dark multitone/monochrome/unmeasured logos, Linux/macOS Sports checks and WGT source compilation. Android is the packaged preview; LG/Samsung packages are not built or device-tested here. Please approve this build before the product PR is updated.
